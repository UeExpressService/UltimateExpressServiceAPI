About Us

Your Trusted Trucking Partner in the Philippines

Ultimate Express is a proudly Filipino-owned trucking company serving businesses nationwide since 2012. We understand the unique challenges of Philippine logistics — from navigating provincial roads to coordinating inter-island Ro-Ro shipments — and we provide reliable, end-to-end transport solutions that keep your supply chain moving.

Our Mission

To deliver consistent excellence in every shipment by providing safe, timely, and cost-effective trucking and logistics services that empower Filipino businesses to grow. We are committed to bridging the distances across the Philippine archipelago, ensuring that every cargo — big or small — arrives at its destination with care, precision, and reliability.

Our Vision

To become the Philippines' most trusted and leading trucking and logistics provider, setting the national standard for reliability, innovation, and service quality in land freight transportation. We aspire to build a seamless logistics network connecting every province, city, and municipality — driving economic growth and opportunity for all Filipinos.

Our Values

Rooted in integrity, reliability, and malasakit — a distinctly Filipino sense of genuine care for our clients, our team, and the communities we serve. We uphold transparency in every transaction, prioritize safety in every journey, and commit to accountability, respect, and continuous improvement in all that we do.
