FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a quotation?

Call or email our team using the contact details on this page. If you already have a customer account, sign in to submit a quote request. Dispatch will review your shipment and confirm the quotation with you.

What details are needed for a quotation?

Prepare the pickup and delivery addresses, cargo description, approximate weight, preferred date and required truck type, if known.

Which truck types can I choose?

Our fleet includes 4-Wheeler L300, 6-Wheeler Closed Van, 10-Wheeler Wing Van, Flatbed Truck, Open Truck, Partner Box Truck and Refrigerated Truck. Dispatch will confirm the suitable truck and its availability for your requested date.

How is the delivery price determined?

Dispatch reviews the route distance, cargo, truck type and any additional charges before confirming your quotation. A published starting amount is a reference; the final price must be agreed with Dispatch.

Does requesting a quote confirm my booking?

No. A quote request does not reserve a truck. Dispatch must confirm the price, truck availability and schedule before your booking is confirmed.

Do I need an account to read the FAQ or contact you?

No account is needed to read these answers or contact us by phone or email. When you need a customer account, our Manager will arrange it and send your initial sign-in details by email.
