Privacy Policy

Ultimate Express operates this website for trucking and logistics services. This page explains how we use information from account inquiries, bookings and the customer portal.

Information we collect

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, company details and messages provided when you request an account or quote.
  • Pickup and delivery addresses, cargo details, booking dates, dispatch assignments and booking status.
  • Quotes, confirmed payment amounts and documents attached to bookings, including waybills.
  • Account and activity records, including sign-ins and changes made to bookings, fleet records and payments.

How we use this information

We use your information to respond to inquiries, set up accounts, prepare quotes, arrange deliveries and provide booking updates. We also use records to confirm payments, prepare reports, troubleshoot the service and track changes.

Google Gmail access

The company authorizes its Gmail sender account with the gmail.send permission. We use this permission to send account setup emails, sign-in codes, password reset links and service notifications. Google receives the recipient address and message content needed to deliver these emails. The application stores the sender's authorization in private server settings so it can send messages when needed.

The mailbox owner can manage or revoke this authorization in their Google Account connections. Revoking it stops the website from sending through that account until the owner authorizes it again. See the Google API Services User Data Policy for Google's rules on this use of its APIs.

When using or transferring Google API data, Ultimate Express follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who can receive the information

Authorized Ultimate Express staff can access records needed for their roles. Customers can view their own bookings and waybills. Railway hosts the online application and database. Google delivers emails when Gmail is the configured provider. Quote notifications may go to the contact provided for the booking and the company inbox.

Route previews send pickup and delivery addresses to OpenStreetMap's geocoding service and route points to the routing service. Opening directions in Google Maps sends the addresses to Google Maps.

Storage and retention

The website uses passwords and email codes for sign-in and limits access by account role. Passwords are stored as hashes for newly created and updated accounts. The database and its backups may contain account, booking, payment and waybill records. Ultimate Express has not set a fixed retention period for bookings and waybills. Contact us if you want to ask how long a specific record will be kept.

Privacy questions and requests

Email ultimateexpresswebservice@gmail.com to ask about your information or request access, a correction or removal. Include enough detail for us to identify the relevant account or booking. Keep passwords and sign-in codes private.
